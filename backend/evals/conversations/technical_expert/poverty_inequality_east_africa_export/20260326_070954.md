## 6. Notes on comparability and traceability

- **National vs international poverty:**  
  - `SI.POV.NAHC` (national line) is **not comparable across countries**; use it for within‑Kenya trends and national strategy monitoring.  
  - PIP headcount (`WB_PIP_HEADCOUNT_IPL`) is designed for **cross‑country comparability** at a fixed PPP line.
- **MPM vs UNDP MPI:**  
  - WB MPM (`SI.POV.MPWB`) includes a **monetary dimension at $3/day**; UNDP MPI (`SI.POV.MPUN`) does not. Don’t mix them in a single series without clear labeling.
- **Inequality indicators:**  
  - Gini and income shares are based on **household surveys**; years correspond to survey years and can be irregular.  
  - Methodological notes in metadata (e.g., use of consumption vs income) are important if you’re doing rigorous inference.

## 6. Notes on comparability and traceability

- **National vs international poverty:**  
  - `SI.POV.NAHC` (national line) is **not comparable across countries**; use it for within‑Kenya trends and national strategy monitoring.  
  - PIP headcount (`WB_PIP_HEADCOUNT_IPL`) is designed for **cross‑country comparability** at a fixed PPP line.
- **MPM vs UNDP MPI:**  
  - WB MPM (`SI.POV.MPWB`) includes a **monetary dimension at $3/day**; UNDP MPI (`SI.POV.MPUN`) does not. Don’t mix them in a single series without clear labeling.
- **Inequality indicators:**  
  - Gini and income shares are based on **household surveys**; years correspond to survey years and can be irregular.
