## 1. Core WDI indicators (India, SDG 6-aligned)

These are from **World Development Indicators (WDI)** and are the most commonly used for SDG 6.1 and 6.2 monitoring.

### 1.1 Drinking water – safely managed (SDG 6.1.1)

- **Indicator name (WDI):** People using safely managed drinking water services (% of population)  
- **Data360 ID:** `WB_WDI_SH_H2O_SMDW_ZS`  
- **Short code (WDI):** `SH.H2O.SMDW.ZS`  
- **Database:** World Development Indicators (WDI)  
- **Unit:** % of population  
- **Periodicity:** Annual  
- **Time coverage:** 2000–2024 (India included)  
- **Concept / definition:**  
  - Share of population using drinking water from an **improved source** that is:
    - accessible on premises,  
    - available when needed, and  
    - free from faecal and priority chemical contamination.  
  - Improved sources: piped water, boreholes/tubewells, protected dug wells, protected springs, packaged or delivered water.  
  - Corresponds to **SDG 6.1.1**.
- **Methodology (summary):**  
  - Produced by the **WHO/UNICEF Joint Monitoring Programme (JMP)**.  
  - Based on household surveys (DHS, MICS, etc.), censuses, and administrative data.  
  - JMP applies **linear regression** to estimate service levels, with rules for interpolation/extrapolation described in the JMP methodology report.
- **Key limitations / caveats:**  
  - Many countries (incl. India, in some years) lack data on one or more of the three criteria (access, availability, quality).  
  - National estimates are only produced when there is data on water quality and at least one of access/availability.  
  - Regional/income-group aggregates only when ≥30% of population coverage.  
  - Methodological switches between using rural/urban aggregation vs. total country estimates can introduce **discontinuities in time series**.
- **Source organization:**  
  - WHO/UNICEF Joint Monitoring Programme (JMP) for Water Supply, Sanitation and Hygiene (washdata.org)  
  - Publisher in WDI: World Bank (aggregating JMP data)
- **Files & APIs:**

- **Country code for India:** `IND` (REF_AREA = `IND` in Data360 APIs; `country=IND` in WDI APIs).

### 3.2 Disaggregation

- The WDI versions above are **totals** only (`SEX=_T`, `AGE=_T`, `URBANISATION=_T`).  
- In the HNP dataset, some indicators list `URBANISATION` options (`_T`, `RUR`, `URB`), so you can pull urban/rural series if needed.

### 3.3 Licensing

- WDI indicators: **CC BY-4.0**  
- HNP indicators: **CC BY 4.0** (with the World Bank’s standard arbitration clause).  
- This generally allows redistribution and integration into internal pipelines, with attribution.
