- **What it measures:**  
  Number of women who die from pregnancy‑related causes while pregnant or within 42 days of termination, per 100,000 live births, based on a **regression model** (not raw registration data).

  - Produced by the **Maternal Mortality Estimation Inter‑Agency Group (MMEIG)**: WHO, UNICEF, UNFPA, World Bank Group, UN DESA/Population Division.

  - Designed to reconcile incomplete or inconsistent country data and produce a time series.

- **Coverage & periodicity:**

- **Limitations / caveats:**  
  - Methodology differs from older estimation rounds; **do not compare to pre‑MMEIG historical series** from other sources.  
  - Ratios are “best estimates” with uncertainty; underlying vital registration and survey data are often weak, even in some high‑income settings.

## 5. Composite: UHC RMNCH service coverage sub‑index

To summarize RMNCH service coverage in one metric:

- **Database:** World Development Indicators (WDI)  
- **Indicator ID (Data360):** `WB_WDI_SH_UHC_SCI_RMNCH`  
- **Name:** UHC service coverage sub‑index on reproductive, maternal, newborn and child health  
- **Unit:** Index (0–100)  

- **What it measures:**  
  A sub‑index of the UHC service coverage index focusing on RMNCH, built from four tracer indicators:  
  1. **Family planning** – % of women 15–49 whose need for family planning is satisfied with modern methods (SDG 3.7.1).  
  2. **Pregnancy care** – % of women 15–49 with a live birth who received ≥4 ANC visits.  
  3. **Child immunization** – % of infants receiving 3 doses of DTP.  
  4. **Child treatment** – % of under‑5 children with symptoms of acute respiratory infection for whom advice/treatment was sought.

- **Methodology:**  
  - Each tracer scaled to 0–100 (0 = worst observed/feasible, 100 = target).  
  - Sub‑index is a **weighted geometric mean** of the four tracers; weights based on the relevant population denominators (using UN population estimates).  
  - Underlies the overall UHC service coverage index (SDG 3.8.1).

- **Coverage & periodicity:**  
  - **Years:** 2000–2023  
  - **Frequency:** Annual  
  - **Unit:** 0–100 index points  
  - **Geographic coverage:** Vietnam (VNM), Thailand (THA), most countries, and regional/income aggregates.

## 5. Composite: UHC RMNCH service coverage sub‑index

To summarize RMNCH service coverage in one metric:

- **Database:** World Development Indicators (WDI)  
- **Indicator ID (Data360):** `WB_WDI_SH_UHC_SCI_RMNCH`  
- **Name:** UHC service coverage sub‑index on reproductive, maternal, newborn and child health  
- **Unit:** Index (0–100)  

- **What it measures:**  
  A sub‑index of the UHC service coverage index focusing on RMNCH, built from four tracer indicators:  
  1. **Family planning** – % of women 15–49 whose need for family planning is satisfied with modern methods (SDG 3.7.1).  
  2. **Pregnancy care** – % of women 15–49 with a live birth who received ≥4 ANC visits.  
  3. **Child immunization** – % of infants receiving 3 doses of DTP.  
  4. **Child treatment** – % of under‑5 children with symptoms of acute respiratory infection for whom advice/treatment was sought.

- **Methodology:**  
  - Each tracer scaled to 0–100 (0 = worst observed/feasible, 100 = target).  
  - Sub‑index is a **weighted geometric mean** of the four tracers; weights based on the relevant population denominators (using UN population estimates).  
  - Underlies the overall UHC service coverage index (SDG 3.8.1).

- **Coverage & periodicity:**  
  - **Years:** 2000–2023  
  - **Frequency:** Annual  
  - **Unit:** 0–100 index points  
  - **Geographic coverage:** Vietnam (VNM), Thailand (THA), most countries, and regional/income aggregates.

- **Download / API:**  
  - CSV (Data360 / WDI):
