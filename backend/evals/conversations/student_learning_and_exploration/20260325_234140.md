**🧠 Thinking** (stage: interpreting)

“Literacy rate” is just a way of saying: *out of 100 people in a certain group, how many can read and write a simple sentence and understand what it means?*

In simple terms:

- **“Literate” person**:  
  Someone who can:
  - read a short, simple text (for example, a short paragraph in a newspaper or a sign), and  
  - write a short, simple sentence  
  - and **understand** what they are reading and writing.

- **“Literacy rate”**:  
  A percentage that answers the question:  
  > “If we take 100 people in this group, how many of them are literate?”

  For example:
  - A literacy rate of 80% means about 80 out of every 100 people can read and write with understanding.
