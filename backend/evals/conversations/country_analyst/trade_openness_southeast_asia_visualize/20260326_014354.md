## Data: Key indicators for trade openness & integration

From World Bank World Development Indicators (WDI), WTO-based tariff data, and related sources:

1. **Overall trade openness (goods & services)**
   - **Indicator:** `Trade (% of GDP)` — **ID:** `WB_WDI_NE_TRD_GNFS_ZS` (Database: `WB_WDI`)
   - **Concept:** Trade = exports + imports of goods and services, as % of GDP.
   - **Definition / methodology (summary):**
     - Trade is the sum of exports and imports of goods and services, expressed as a percentage of GDP.
     - National accounts (GDP) follow the **System of National Accounts (SNA 1993/2008)**. Balance of payments and merchandise trade follow IMF/BPM and UN trade standards.
   - **Periodicity:** Annual.
   - **Coverage:** Most countries globally, from 1960–2024.
   - **Comparability notes:**
     - Countries are broadly comparable because they follow common SNA and balance-of-payments standards, but:
       - Some use SNA 1993 vs SNA 2008; rebasing and methodological upgrades can cause level shifts.
       - Revisions to balance-of-payments manuals (BPM5 vs BPM6) can affect services and thus total trade.
     - For regional comparisons, it’s usually safe, but mention that differences in the coverage of informal trade and data quality can affect low‑income countries.

2. **Goods trade openness**
   - **Indicator:** `Merchandise trade (% of GDP)` — **ID:** `WB_WDI_TG_VAL_TOTL_GD_ZS` (Database: `WB_WDI`)
   - **Concept:** Merchandise exports + merchandise imports, as % of GDP.
   - **Definition / methodology (summary):**
     - “General merchandise trade” covers goods whose **economic ownership changes** between a resident and a non‑resident, excluding:
       - Goods under merchanting,
       - Non‑monetary gold,
       - Parts of travel, construction, and government goods and services not elsewhere included.
     - Expressed as % of GDP (same GDP concept as above).
     - Trade statistics compiled per **International Merchandise Trade Statistics – Concepts and Definitions 2010 (IMTS 2010)**.
   - **Periodicity:** Annual.
   - **Coverage:** Wide coverage from 1960–2024 for most economies.
   - **Comparability notes:**
     - Good cross‑country comparability for formal goods trade; all countries align to IMTS 2010, but:
       - Treatment of re‑exports, processing trade, and free zones can differ.
       - Informal cross‑border trade often under‑recorded (important for some ASEAN economies).
     - GDP denominator is consistent with national accounts standards but may differ in base year.

3. **Services trade openness**
   - **Indicator:** `Trade in services (% of GDP)` — **ID:** `WB_WDI_BG_GSR_NFSV_GD_ZS` (Database: `WB_WDI`)
   - **Concept:** Exports + imports of services, as % of GDP.
   - **Definition / methodology (summary):**
     - Total trade in services includes:
       - Services provided by residents to non‑residents, plus
       - Services provided by non‑residents to residents.
     - Expressed as % of GDP.
     - Based on **Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM5/BPM6)**.
   - **Periodicity:** Annual.
   - **Coverage:** Broad coverage 1960–2024, but many developing countries have shorter and less complete series.
   - **Comparability notes:**
     - Methodology is harmonized via BPM, but:

   - **Concept:** Average applied tariff on merchandise, weighted by import values.
   - **Definition / methodology (summary):**
     - **Weighted mean applied tariff** = average of effectively applied tariff rates, weighted by each product’s share in total imports.
     - Data:
       - Tariffs classified in **Harmonized System (HS)** at 6– or 8‑digit level.
       - Mapped to **SITC Rev. 3** to define commodity groups and import weights.

       - Specific (non‑ad valorem) rates are converted to **ad valorem equivalents** where possible.
     - Tariff data sourced primarily from **WTO** (CTS and IDB), complemented by **UNCTAD**.
     - Typically updated annually, with a 1–2 year publication lag.
   - **Periodicity:** Annual.
   - **Coverage:** Many countries from 1988–2022.
   - **Comparability notes:**
     - Highly comparable across countries due to:
       - Common HS classification,
       - Consistent weighting using Comtrade.
     - But:

       - Tariff peaks and non‑ad valorem rates are approximated via ad valorem equivalents, which can introduce measurement error.
       - Only **statutory tariffs**; does not capture non‑tariff measures (NTMs), trade remedies, or implementation gaps.
