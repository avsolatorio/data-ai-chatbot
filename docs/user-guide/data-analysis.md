# Data Analysis

Data360 Chat can help you explore development data: search indicators, view metadata, fetch time-series data, and generate charts. The AI uses the Data360 platform through integrated tools.

---

## What you can do

- **Search indicators** — Find indicators by topic, country, or keyword
- **View metadata** — Get details about indicators (definition, source, disaggregation)
- **Fetch data** — Retrieve time-series data for specific indicators and countries
- **Generate charts** — Create visualizations (e.g. line charts, bar charts) from the data

---

## Example questions

- "Find indicators about poverty in Kenya"
- "What indicators are available for education?"
- "Show me GDP growth for Nigeria from 2010 to 2020"
- "Create a chart of infant mortality trends in Sub-Saharan Africa"
- "What is the definition of the GINI index?"

---

## How it works

1. You ask a question in natural language.
2. The AI decides whether to use data tools (search, metadata, data, charts).
3. It may show "thinking" stages while searching or fetching data.
4. Results (e.g. search results, charts) appear as part of the response.
5. Charts are rendered inline; you can interact with them (e.g. hover for values) depending on the implementation.

---

## Tips

- Be specific about countries, time periods, and indicators when possible.
- If the first search does not find what you need, try rephrasing or narrowing the topic.
- You can ask follow-up questions to refine or expand the analysis.
