# Getting Started

This guide helps you sign in and send your first message in Data360 Chat.

---

## Sign up or log in

Data360 Chat supports three ways to sign in:

### Email and password

1. Click **Sign in** or **Register**.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. If registering, complete the form and submit.
4. You will be signed in and redirected to the chat.

### Guest (try without account)

1. Click **Continue as guest**.
2. A temporary session is created. Your chats are saved for the session but may be limited.
3. To save your work long-term, create an account later.

### Microsoft (Azure AD)

If your organization uses Azure AD:

1. Click **Sign in with Microsoft**.
2. Authenticate with your Microsoft account.
3. You will be redirected back to the chat.

---

## Start chatting

1. **Open the app** — You will see the chat interface with a message input at the bottom.
2. **Type your question** — Enter your message in the input box.
3. **Send** — Press Enter or click the send button.
4. **Wait for the response** — The AI streams its response in real time. You may see "thinking" stages when it is searching data or reasoning.

---

## First message ideas

- "What is GDP and how is it measured?"
- "Find indicators about poverty in Kenya"
- "Create a simple Python script to read a CSV file"
- "Help me write an email to my team about the project update"
